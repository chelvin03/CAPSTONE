INCLUDED WORKFLOWS
1. Event outcomes and attendance
   Approved staff/admin record Completed or No-show only after the event ends.
   Completed requires 1-2,000 actual attendees; no-show requires zero. Mandatory
   remarks, recorder, timestamp and revision are kept. Cancelled/unapproved
   requests cannot become no-shows. No automatic event completion occurs.
   Admin corrections require a reason and current revision; old values remain
   in the immutable audit log. Historical schedules with recorded outcomes or
   returns cannot be moved. AR completion remains separate from event completion.

2. Equipment returns and incidents
   After the end, staff/admin record actual issued quantity, good returns,
   damaged and missing quantities. Good + damaged + missing must equal issued,
   which cannot exceed approved quantity. Recording event completion does not
   imply equipment was returned. Admins correct/recover/repair with a reason and
   current revision. Unresolved damaged/missing units reduce usable stock for
   future requests and approval checks; total inventory is not overwritten.
   Existing upcoming allocations that exceed usable stock are flagged for
   admin review on Follow-up & Offers. They are not silently cancelled.
   An existing incident can still be recovered by admin after booking closure.

3. Reminders and follow-up
   Admin sidebar > Follow-up & Offers shows reminders due for:
   - Approved events starting within 24 hours.
   - Public requests still missing a recorded supporting permit after 24 hours.
   - Unanswered replacement proposals after 24 hours, or waiting-list offers
     after 12 hours, while still valid.
   Each reminder shows recipient/message before an explicit Send click.
   Delivery status and attempts are recorded. Already sent reminders for the
   same schedule/recipient/state are blocked. Failed sends can be retried after
   one hour. Stale, expired or cancelled items are rechecked before sending.
   IMPORTANT: reminders are MANUAL on this hosting setup. There is no automatic
   cron/background delivery and no public scheduler URL. A reminder never
   approves, cancels or changes a reservation.

4. Waiting-list offers and replacement acceptance
   Follow-up & Offers lists upcoming waiting requests with priority/submission
   context. Admin opens a request, reviews eligibility/documents, checks the
   review acknowledgement, provides a reason and previews the offer.
   A signed private email link requires an explicit Accept/Decline POST.
   GET/email scanning never applies an offer. No proposed slot is held.
   Waiting offers expire after 24 hours or at proposed start, whichever is
   first. Acceptance rechecks slot, cleaning gap, blocks, restrictions and
   equipment under locks, then confirms approval and creates the AR task.
   Competing offers cannot displace the winner. Declining/expiry keeps the
   request waiting. Admin chooses recipients; there is no automatic promotion.
   Ordinary replacement proposals expire after 48 hours or proposed start and
   preserve existing approval status on acceptance. New offers supersede old
   offers; cancelled/changed originals invalidate private links.

5. BI refinement
   Existing charts and insights are retained. Added verified event completion,
   no-show, outcome coverage, attendance comparisons, return records/missing
   returns and incident quantities, with definitions and supporting records.
   Completion/no-show rates use recorded ended outcomes only. Coverage exposes
   missing records; cancelled, rejected, unapproved and future events are not
   assumed to have occurred. Attendance compares the same completed events.
   Additional completion/no-show/coverage KPI targets are configurable only by
   administrator review. No benchmark targets are fabricated or enabled by
   default. Configure agreed MCST targets before the panel demonstration.
   Free-text cancellation email addresses/phone numbers are masked in BI and
   its CSV; the original reason remains in the reservation record for review.
